Quick checks

Before opening an issue.

PERMISSIONS

Confirm macOS access

Private Meetings needs Microphone and Screen & System Audio Recording permission. Screen recording approval can require an app relaunch.

LOCAL MODELS

Check model status

Open Local AI settings and verify the pinned transcription model. Summaries require the separate local summary model.

MICROPHONE

Verify the actual device

Confirm the selected microphone name in the app. Meeting software can use a different device than the macOS system default.

PRIVACY

Do not post meeting data

Never attach recordings, transcripts, API keys, credentials, private hostnames, or confidential meeting details to a public issue.

FAQ

Common questions.

How long can I record?

Live capture stops automatically at 70 minutes and warns at 65 minutes. The pinned MOSS model documents a 90-minute single-pass ceiling; the app keeps twenty minutes of margin for reliable finalization and transcription.

Does Private Meetings identify people by name?

No. It separates speaker turns with anonymous labels such as SPEAKER_01 and SPEAKER_02. It does not perform biometric voice identification or guess a real identity.

Does my meeting leave the Mac?

Not when Local Private AI is active. After the local models are installed, transcription and local summaries run on the Mac. Advanced Remote routes send data only after you configure, enable, and confirm that route.

Why does the app need Screen & System Audio Recording permission?

ScreenCaptureKit uses that permission to capture system audio. Private Meetings does not write screen pixels to disk.

Which microphone is recorded?

The app shows and persists the actual microphone device selected for capture. You can choose the system default, a USB microphone, or a compatible audio interface.

Do local models require internet access?

Internet access is required to download them. The app pins and verifies the expected model files before activation. Once installed, local meeting processing does not require uploading meeting content.

Can I import an existing recording?

Yes. Choose a supported audio or video file. Private Meetings reads the selected source, creates a private audio-only copy, and does not modify the original file.

What does Purge remove?

Purge removes all meetings, recordings, isolated tracks, transcripts, summaries, sidecar metadata, orphaned recording files, and meeting database records. It keeps downloaded AI models, preferences, and Keychain credentials.

Is this a subscription?

No. Private Meetings is preparing for a one-time $4.99 Mac App Store launch.

What macOS version is required?

macOS 15 or later on Apple silicon.

What should I include in a support issue?

Include the app version, macOS version, the feature you were using, what you expected, and the exact non-sensitive error message. Do not post meeting content, credentials, private endpoint details, or personal information.

Protect the meeting while asking for help.

Public support works best with reproducible steps and exact error text—not recordings, transcripts, credentials, or confidential context.
